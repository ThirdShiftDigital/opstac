What's Inside

Everything a tactical team actually uses

Not a general-purpose scheduling app repurposed for law enforcement — built around the specific rhythm of readiness, activation, and after-action review.

Roster & Readiness

Status, on-call availability, equipment assignments, certification expirations, and training hours — one view of who's actually ready right now.

Operations Planning

Upload a real scene image and place operators on it, build a pre-ops plan, run the debrief afterward, and get flagged issues from the built-in analyzer.

Instant Callouts

Activate the team by text, Signal, or share — mark it standby or deploy, set a rally point, and track who's actually acknowledged.

Delegated Command

Give Team Leaders exactly the authority you want — edit operations, run callouts, manage records — controlled per permission, not all-or-nothing.
